About LocalLog

LocalLog is a simple time tracker for your projects. Track time for customers and projects, correct entries right in the table and export them for Excel.

  • Your entries stay in this browser on this computer. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Other browsers and other computers do not see your entries. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so set up a backup folder or export regularly.
  • Once installed, LocalLog also works without an internet connection.
Technical details
  • Storage: entries, customers and projects are kept in the browser's IndexedDB database "locallog".
  • Backup: an optional folder (File System Access API) receives customers.csv, projects.csv, entries.csv and meta.csv. The files use UTF-8, a comma separator, ISO timestamps and decimal points.
  • Network: a Content Security Policy with connect-src 'none' blocks the app from sending data anywhere. The app has no external dependencies and no telemetry.
  • Offline use: a service worker caches the app files. A new version is picked up automatically after an update.
  • Formats: numbers, dates and times follow your browser's regional settings. The interface language can be switched in the header.
